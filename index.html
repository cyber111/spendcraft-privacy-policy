SpendCraft Privacy Policy

By Pratik Jain · Last updated: 29 September 2026

SpendCraft is an expense tracker for Android. By default it runs in Guest mode and stores everything only on your device — nothing is sent to us. If you choose to sign in, your email address and the transactions, budgets and categories you enter are stored in your account so they can be backed up and synced. We don't show ads, use analytics, or sell your data.

1. Data we collect

Guest mode (the default). SpendCraft does not collect any data. Your transactions, budgets, categories and settings are saved only in the app's private storage on your phone and never leave it.

If you sign in (optional), we collect:

  • Email address — to create and secure your account.
  • An account ID — a random identifier that links your data to your account.
  • Your financial entries — the transactions (amount, type, category, note, date), budgets and custom categories you add, so they can be backed up and synced across your devices.

This data is stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider, in an account that only you can access. Like most web services, Supabase keeps short-term technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and secure the service; we don't use them to track or locate you.

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, device identifiers or usage analytics, and we do not use advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.

2. How we use data

  • App functionality — to show, back up and sync your transactions and budgets.
  • Account management — to sign you in and keep your account secure.

We don't use your data for advertising, profiling or analytics.

3. Sharing

We don't share your data with anyone. Supabase processes it only to provide the service to us. We never sell your data.

If you use Export to CSV, the file is created on your device and sent only where you choose through Android's share menu.

4. Storage & security

  • All data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Each account's rows are protected by database row-level security: an account can only read and write its own data.
  • Data on your device lives in the app's private storage and is removed when you uninstall the app.

5. Your rights & deleting your data

  • On your device: Settings → Clear all data removes every transaction and budget stored on the phone. You can also swipe to delete individual transactions.
  • Your account: Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your account and all data stored with it.
  • By email: write to pjpratikjain7@gmail.com from the address on your account and we'll delete your account and all associated data within 30 days. You can also ask for a copy of your data the same way.

6. Children

SpendCraft is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change how SpendCraft handles data, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
